# Score's built-in orchestra
---

The instruments under /soundfonts/orchestra/ (one SF3 file each, listed in
/soundfonts/orchestra.json) are made from two sample libraries by
scripts/sounds/build.py of Score's source. Both may be used and passed on
for any purpose, commercial too.

## VSCO 2 Community Edition 1.1.0

Versilian Studios (Sam Gossner) — https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Every instrument but the piano: the solo violin, the violin, viola and cello
sections, the double bass, the string orchestra (the four put on one
keyboard), harp, piccolo, flute, oboe, clarinet, bassoon, French horn,
trumpet, muted trumpet, trombone, tuba, timpani, glockenspiel, xylophone,
marimba, tubular bells and the pipe organ.

## Salamander Grand Piano V3

Alexander Holm — https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
(a Yamaha C5, recorded by Alexander Holm; this copy of the samples at commit
3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404)
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The grand piano (piano.….sf3).

## What was changed

The samples were converted, not re-recorded:

- the silence before each attack removed;
- held sounds (bowed, blown, the organ, rolls) given a loop with a crossfade,
  and cut at its end; sounds that die away cut where they are 60 dB down, or
  after a few seconds (ten for the piano), and faded;
- samples 4 to 40 cents off their note brought to pitch (not the piano's,
  nor the timpani's or the bells'); the glockenspiel and the xylophone put
  at the octave they sound;
- levels evened out, across each instrument and between its dynamic layers;
- the first of each note's round robins kept; of the piano, three of its
  sixteen dynamic layers (4, 9 and 14), without its release, pedal and
  string-resonance samples;
- each channel compressed as Ogg Vorbis (quality 5) and put in an SF3
  SoundFont, an articulation a preset.

The files made are under the licence of the library they come from: CC0 1.0
for the VSCO 2 instruments, CC BY 3.0 for the piano — credit Alexander Holm.
